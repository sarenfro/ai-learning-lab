The founder opportunity

Most companies want AI.
Few know where to start.

The AI opportunity is real. The pressure from boards, investors, and customers is real. But building an internal AI roadmap, running customer discovery, and prototyping a working product in-house takes time and people most early-stage and PE-backed companies simply don't have.

McKinsey found that only 1% of executives describe their generative AI operations as "mature," and fewer than a third of companies are following the practices that actually drive AI ROI at scale. The window for early movers is open now.

Source: McKinsey, "The state of AI: How organizations are rewiring to capture value," March 2025

What your company gets
  • 01
    A market-sized AI opportunity map

    Industry analysis, competitive landscape, and AI vendor shortlist tailored to your exact use case, presented exec-ready.

  • 02
    A defensible business case

    TAM/SAM/SOM modeling, cost-benefit analysis, and a build vs. buy vs. partner recommendation backed by real numbers.

  • 03
    Real customer validation

    Five to ten interviews with your actual customers or prospects, synthesized into ICP definition and demand signal documentation.

  • 04
    A working AI prototype

    Not a wireframe. An actual, prompt-engineered, tested AI POC your team can hand off to engineering or take to your next board meeting.

  • 05
    A go-to-market launch plan

    Launch sequencing, pricing signals, early adopter strategy, and a cross-functional RACI ready for your team to execute.

For founders and operators

You're the right company if...

  • ✓You're a PE-backed or venture-funded software company with a real AI use case to explore
  • ✓You want a working prototype, not a 50-page strategic memo
  • ✓You're willing to give students access to real data, real customers, and real decision-makers
  • ✓You want to evaluate top MBA talent for potential paid or full-time continuation
  • ✓You believe early movers win, and you want to be one of them
  • ✗You want a light-touch advisory engagement with no real deliverable expectations
For MBA students

You're the right student if...

  • ✓You want operator experience, not just a case study on your resume
  • ✓You're curious about AI in a business context (no coding required)
  • ✓You're comfortable with ambiguity and fast iteration under a senior mentor
  • ✓You want direct access to PE-level operators and portfolio company leadership
  • ✓You want a path to paid continuation after graduation
  • ✗You're looking for a light-touch course to cruise through your final semester
